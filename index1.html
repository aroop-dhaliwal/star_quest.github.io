Mission: on foot, press E to grab each crate and drop them at the gold marker by the jet while passengers board. Then press E by the jet to climb in and fly to the gold beacon (the destination changes every run). You earn $1,000 per crate and $2,000 per passenger delivered, and can spend it on upgrades on this screen (cargo belt and travellator unlock for $10,000, then $5,000 per upgrade; other upgrades $5,000). On foot: W/S walk, A/D turn.

You can land gear-down on any flat ground, gently (runways are easiest), but not in water: lakes and rivers are blue. Mountains and buildings are solid. Cyan beacons mark airports. Day and night swap every 5 minutes.


Touch mode: left joystick = throttle in flight, walking on foot. Arrow buttons steer and climb. E and GEAR are bottom-right.

Press any key (or tap) to start on the runway
